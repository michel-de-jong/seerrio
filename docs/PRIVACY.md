# Seerr Mobile privacy policy

_Last updated: October 22, 2026_

Seerr Mobile is an unofficial client for [Seerr](https://github.com/seerr-team/seerr), a request manager you or someone in your household hosts yourselves. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Seerr project.

## The short version

The developer of Seerr Mobile does not collect, receive, store, sell, or share any of your data. The app has no accounts of its own, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no crash reporting.

## Who the app talks to

The app connects only to:

* **The Seerr server you enter.** Your email address, password, requests, issues, comments, and profile settings go to that server, because that is how Seerr works. The server's owner controls that data under their own policies. If you use an `http://` address instead of `https://`, this traffic is not encrypted, and the app warns you before you connect.
* **`image.tmdb.org`**, to load movie and series posters. These requests reveal which images are being loaded and your IP address to The Movie Database (TMDB), under [TMDB's privacy policy](https://www.themoviedb.org/privacy-policy).

If you choose to open a title in your media library, the app asks first and then hands the link to your phone's browser or media app.

## What is stored on your phone

These items are stored only on your device, in the iOS Keychain or Android Keystore-backed secure storage, and are never sent to the developer:

* The address of your Seerr server.
* Your Seerr session cookie, or the API key if you use API-key sign-in. Unless you turn on "Stay signed in", these are kept only while the app is open.
* A copy of your Seerr user profile, so the app can show your name and permissions.
* Your appearance choice (System, Light, or Dark).

The app never stores your password. Your phone's password manager may offer to save it; that is controlled by you and your phone, not the app.

Signing out removes the session, the API key, and the cached profile. Deleting the app removes everything it stored.

## Children

Seerr Mobile is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.
